\section{Conclusion}
We presented an efficient, training-free post-processing method to resolve collisions in 3D scenes generated by diffusion models. Using an alignment-biased SAT resolver, we seamlessly separate penetrating objects and strictly enforce room containment while preserving the global layout. Experiments on DiffuScene~\cite{tang2024diffuscenedenoisingdiffusionmodels} demonstrate drastic collision reductions without sacrificing generative quality, offering a practical solution for physically grounded scene synthesis.

\textbf{Limitations and Future Work.} While our geometric approach is highly efficient and model-agnostic, it operates independently of semantic priors. Consequently, resolving a collision may slightly shift an object from its semantically optimal position, though our alignment-biased axis selection minimizes this disruption. Moving forward, this robust module can be integrated into an automated generation pipeline. By guaranteeing physically plausible, collision-free layouts, our approach bridges the gap between raw generative AI outputs and ready-to-use environments for downstream applications like VR, AR, and interactive 3D simulations.
